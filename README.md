# Stranger Things

A popular science fiction horror drama television series created by the Duffer Brothers.

## Quick Overview
* **Genre:** Sci-Fi, Horror, Mystery
* **Setting:** Hawkins, Indiana (1980s)
* **Themes:** Friendship, supernatural forces, government conspiracies

---

### Key Characters
1. **Eleven** - A young girl with psychokinetic abilities.
2. **Mike Wheeler** - A smart and dedicated friend who helps shelter Eleven.
3. **Jim Hopper** - The chief of Hawkins Police Department.

### Why It's My Favorite
> "Friends don't lie." This core rule drives the entire group through their fights against the Upside Down. The series perfectly blends 80s nostalgia with gripping suspense and unforgettable characters.
